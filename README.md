# Power BI Development 
With nearly 30 years of experience in the hospitality industry — including senior roles in city hotels, luxury resorts, and international chains — I have built a career grounded in operational leadership, financial strategy, and data-informed decision-making.

As a Food & Beverage Manager since 2010, I've overseen multimillion-euro budgets, designed daily workflow pipelines, led cross-functional teams, and implemented reporting systems that improved performance and accountability across departments.

Recognising the growing importance of analytics, I completed a one-year specialization in Data Analysis at the University of Athens. I've since built interactive dashboards and end-to-end BI solutions using Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, applying ETL, semantic modeling, DAX, relational databases and dimensional modeling — always grounded in real-world hospitality operations.

These portfolio projects reflect my ability to translate complex operational data into actionable insights — from focused Power BI dashboards to end-to-end Microsoft Fabric solutions — combining technical proficiency with deep hospitality business knowledge.
